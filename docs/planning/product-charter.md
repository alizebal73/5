# Product Charter

GameNet 5 targets a single physical GameNet/cybercafe first.

First usable product: authentication/permissions; station/device management; customer/session lifecycle; tariff resolution; billing/wallet/debt; inventory/buffet; VIP; audit; backup/recovery; update/rollback; operator diagnostics.

Out of first scope: multi-tenant SaaS, microservices for their own sake, browser operator UI, client-side authoritative state, and unmeasured distributed infrastructure.
